What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is a voice assistant that answers your business phone 24/7. It talks to callers, answers common questions, takes their details, books appointments into your calendar and sends you a short summary of every call, so no customer is lost to voicemail.

How long does it take to set up an AI receptionist or chatbot?

Most AI receptionist and chatbot setups take about one week: a short call to learn your business, setup and testing, then go-live. Custom systems such as a booking platform or CRM usually take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope.

How much does a custom AI or software project cost?

Every project gets a fixed price after a short call, so there are no surprise invoices. Smaller AI receptionist and chatbot setups are a one-time setup fee plus a small monthly fee for hosting and support; larger custom systems are quoted per project.

Which businesses do you work with?

Mostly small and mid-sized businesses that run on phone calls, bookings or quotes: clinics and pharmacies, real estate firms, e-commerce brands, manufacturers and local service businesses, in the US and Europe.

Can the AI book appointments into my existing calendar or system?

Yes. The AI receptionist and chatbots can connect to Google Calendar, booking tools, CRMs and custom databases, and can hand a conversation over to a real person when needed.
