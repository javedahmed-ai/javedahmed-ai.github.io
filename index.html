Questions people actually ask

What is an AI receptionist, exactly?

A phone number connected to an AI model instead of a person. It picks up, has a real back-and-forth with the caller, writes down what they need, and puts it on your calendar. You get a short summary afterward instead of a missed-call notification.

How long before something's actually live?

An AI receptionist or chatbot is usually about a week — one call to understand the business, then setup and testing. A full booking platform or CRM runs 3 to 8 weeks depending on what it needs to do.

What does this cost?

Depends on scope, so I quote after a call rather than guessing here. Receptionists and chatbots are usually a setup fee plus a small monthly amount for hosting; bigger systems are priced per project.

Who do you usually build for?

Small and mid-size businesses where the bottleneck is calls, bookings or quotes — clinics, pharmacies, real estate offices, online stores, manufacturers.

Can it plug into what I already use?

Usually — Google Calendar, an existing booking tool, a CRM, or a database I build for you. It can also hand off to a real person when it should.

Is customer data actually safe?

Access is locked down per role, connections are encrypted, and everything's backed up. I can host in the EU if GDPR applies to you. Every screenshot on this page is demo data — no client's real information is ever shown.

Do you stick around after launch?

Yes. I host, monitor and fix these myself. If something breaks, I'm the one who finds out and the one who fixes it.
